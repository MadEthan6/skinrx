Wondering what happens at a skincare or medical aesthetics consultation? Here is exactly what to expect at SkinRx, from skin analysis to your personalized treatment plan.

Every natural-looking result begins not with a treatment, but with a conversation.

The consultation is the most important appointment you will have with us, and often the most reassuring. It is where guesswork ends and a clear, personalized plan begins. If you have never visited a medical aesthetics practice, or if you have only tried products off a shelf hoping for the best, here is a step-by-step look at what happens when you walk through our doors.

Before You Arrive

There is no elaborate preparation required, and no need to come in knowing which specific treatment you want. Our job is to listen, evaluate, and guide you to what makes clinical sense for your face and goals.

Step One: Understanding You and Your Goals

We begin by sitting down and talking. What do you notice in the mirror that bothers you? What features do you love and want to preserve? Have you had treatments before, and what worked or did not work? Whether you want subtle refinement, help with a specific concern, or a timeline ahead of a wedding or milestone, your priorities shape every recommendation. There are no wrong answers and zero sales pressure.

Step Two: A Thorough Skin Assessment

Next, your provider examines your skin closely and professionally. We look beyond the surface concern to evaluate your skin type and tone, surface texture, sun damage, volume and facial structure, and the underlying drivers behind what brought you in.

Two patients with similar concerns can need completely different approaches. Getting the clinical assessment right is something a product label or online quiz simply cannot replace.

Step Three: Your Personalized Treatment Plan

With a clear picture of both your goals and your skin, we build your plan from our full clinical menu. That might include a foundation of medical-grade skincare (SkinBetter Science or Glytone), resurfacing treatments such as chemical peels, PRX, RF microneedling, Exion, or EmFace, injectable options like neurotoxins, dermal fillers, Sculptra, Radiesse, or EZ Gel, or prescription support when appropriate. Often, it is a thoughtful combination sequenced over time.

We explain why we recommend each element, what it targets, and how the pieces work together so you leave understanding the reasoning behind every step.

Step Four: Honest Expectations and Transparent Costs

A thorough consultation is honest about timelines, realistic outcomes, downtime, aftercare, and maintenance. Some treatments deliver quick refinement, while collagen-building options unfold gradually over weeks and months.

We also review exact pricing up front so there are no surprises, including Cherry Payment Plan monthly options if you prefer to spread an investment over time.

Step Five: Your Questions and Next Steps

Before we wrap up, the floor is yours. No question is too small, and there is never any obligation to start treatment on the spot. Many patients take their plan home to think it over and return when they feel ready. When you decide to move forward, we map out scheduling or a home skincare routine together.

How to Prepare for Your Consultation

  • Arrive with clean skin or ready to remove makeup so your skin can be assessed accurately.
  • Bring a list of your current products along with any medications or supplements.
  • Note past aesthetic treatments, especially injectables or energy procedures, and roughly when you had them.
  • Jot down your questions and goals beforehand so nothing slips your mind.
  • Come open-minded. Sometimes a simpler or more targeted option is a better fit than what you originally pictured.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation obligation-free?

Yes. The consultation is dedicated to understanding your skin and building the right plan, with zero pressure to book a procedure on the spot.

Will I be treated on the same day?

Sometimes, when clinically appropriate and when you feel ready, certain treatments can be performed the same day. Others are scheduled separately to allow proper preparation or numbing.

What should I bring?

Bring a list of your current skincare products, relevant medications, and notes on past treatments, along with your questions and goals.

How long does a consultation take?

Plan for an unhurried appointment. We take the time needed to evaluate your skin carefully and answer every question.

Do I need to stop using any products beforehand?

For the consultation itself, usually no. If a specific peel, microneedling, or laser treatment is scheduled afterward, we will give you clear pre-treatment instructions.

Ready to Begin?

The hardest part of any skin journey is often simply taking the first step. A consultation is low-pressure, genuinely informative, and the start of a plan built around you.
