Volume loss, fine lines, laxity: discover how neurotoxins, Sculptra, dermal fillers, EZ Gel, EmFace, Exion, PRX, and RF microneedling work together to refresh aging skin. A guide from SkinRx.

Aging is not a flaw to be erased; it is a process to be guided. The most natural results in aesthetic medicine never look "done." They look like you, simply rested, refreshed, and well-cared-for.

The key is understanding that facial aging unfolds in layers, each calling for a different approach. The art lies in matching the right layer with the right treatment and combining them with restraint.

How the Face Actually Ages

Time changes the face on several levels at once:

  • Skin quality declines: Collagen and elastin diminish steadily from our mid-twenties onward. Combined with cumulative UV exposure, skin becomes thinner, drier, less even, and more lined.
  • Volume is lost: Facial fat pads and deeper structural support deflate and shift over time, hollowing cheeks and temples, deepening folds, and softening jawline definition.
  • Muscles change: Repeated expression etches dynamic lines into the skin, while deeper lifting muscles gradually lose tone.

A complete, subtle plan addresses all three movements: relaxing overactive pull, restoring lost support, and rebuilding the skin itself.

Relaxing Dynamic Lines

Neurotoxins (Dysport, Xeomin & Letybo)

The lines that form when you frown, squint, or raise your brows (such as the "11s" between the eyes, forehead creases, and crow's feet) come from repeated muscle contraction. Neuromodulators gently relax those targeted muscles, softening lines while preserving natural expression.

Restoring Lost Volume

Where time has deflated structural support, thoughtful restoration comes in three distinct options:

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid fillers restore immediate volume and contour, supporting hollowed cheeks and temples, softening folds, and refining lip and jawline balance with precise, reversible results.

Sculptra

Sculptra is a poly-L-lactic acid collagen biostimulator that prompts your skin to rebuild its own collagen gradually over a series of sessions. Results emerge subtly over months and are notably long-lasting for foundational facial support.

EZ Gel

For patients drawn to a 100% autologous option, EZ Gel is a natural biofiller made entirely from your own blood. Processed into a smooth gel rich in platelets, fibrin, and growth factors, it gently restores delicate areas like under-eye hollows and temples while stimulating collagen over time.

Rebuilding the Skin Itself

Surface firmness, hydration, texture, and tone live in the skin's deeper architecture. These treatments regenerate that foundation:

EmFace

EmFace is a needle-free treatment that tones facial lifting muscles with HIFES energy while heating deeper skin layers with synchronized radiofrequency. Over a short series of 20-minute sessions with zero downtime, it creates natural lift across the brows, cheeks, and jawline.

Exion

Exion pairs monopolar radiofrequency with targeted ultrasound, guided by AI, to reach deep into the dermis and stimulate your skin's own hyaluronic acid, collagen, and elastin production without needles on the facial applicator.

RF Microneedling

BTL Exion RF microneedling pairs micro-channels with controlled radiofrequency heat in the deep dermis, firming lax skin, refining pores and texture, and softening fine lines and crepiness across a 3-session series.

PRX Derm Perfexion

PRX is a needle-free biorevitalization treatment ("no-peel peel") formulated with trichloroacetic acid, hydrogen peroxide, and kojic acid to firm and brighten deeper layers without surface peeling or downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best treatment for aging skin?

There is no single universal treatment because aging happens across muscle, volume, and skin quality layers. The most natural outcomes usually combine two or three complementary modalities tailored to your face.

What is the difference between fillers, Sculptra, and EZ Gel?

Hyaluronic acid fillers provide immediate, reversible contour. Sculptra rebuilds your own collagen gradually for multi-year durability. EZ Gel uses your own blood components for soft, natural support in delicate areas like under the eyes.

Are non-invasive options like EmFace and Exion as effective as injectables?

They perform different roles. EmFace and Exion strengthen muscle tone, firmness, and skin quality without needles, while injectables relax dynamic lines or restore structural volume. They work especially well together.

When will I see results, and how long do they last?

Neurotoxins and HA fillers appear within days to two weeks. Biostimulators and energy-based collagen treatments build gradually over weeks to months and tend to last longer.

Is there any downtime?

Options like EmFace, Exion Face, and PRX carry little to no downtime, while injectables or RF microneedling may involve 1 to 3 days of mild redness, swelling, or occasional bruising.
