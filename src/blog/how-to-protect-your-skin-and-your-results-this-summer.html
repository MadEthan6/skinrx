Summer in Iowa brings long days outside, lake weekends, and county fairs. Yet if you have been investing in your skin through aesthetic treatments, summer UV exposure is the single biggest threat to protecting those results. Here is what you need to know to keep your skin healthy, bright, and protected all summer long.

Why Sun Exposure Undoes Your Medspa Results

UV radiation is responsible for up to 80% of visible facial aging, including wrinkles, dark spots, uneven tone, and collagen breakdown. Every treatment you invest in, from neurotoxins and fillers to RF microneedling, Exion, and chemical peels, works to restore and strengthen your skin. Unprotected sun exposure pushes directly against that progress.

UV rays break down collagen, darken hyperpigmentation, and create inflammation that can reverse the gains of resurfacing procedures. After a peel, microneedling, or laser session, fresh skin is especially vulnerable for several weeks.

The Non-Negotiable: Broad-Spectrum SPF Every Single Day

This is the single most impactful daily habit for your skin, more important than any single office procedure: wear broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher every morning, rain or shine, indoors or out. UVA rays pass through clouds and glass windows, so daily application matters even on errands or office days.

Post-Treatment Sun Rules to Follow

  • Chemical peels: Avoid direct sun exposure for at least 1 to 2 weeks while freshly resurfaced skin heals.
  • RF Microneedling: Avoid prolonged sun exposure for 1 week post-treatment and wear SPF 30+ daily for at least 4 weeks.
  • Laser hair removal: Avoid tanning or direct sun on treated areas for 2 weeks before and after each session.
  • Injectables (Dysport, Xeomin, Letybo, fillers): No strict sun restriction, though daily SPF preserves collagen and helps overall skin quality last longer.
  • EmFace & Exion: Mild sun sensitivity is possible for a few days afterward, so daily SPF remains essential.

Our Top Summer Skincare Tips

  • Apply SPF as the final step of your morning skincare routine every day.
  • Reapply every 2 hours when outdoors and immediately after swimming or sweating.
  • Wear a wide-brimmed hat and UV-protective clothing during extended outdoor days.
  • Use a gentle, non-stripping cleanser to clear sweat, sunscreen, and summer oil without irritating your barrier.
  • Pair daily sunscreen with an antioxidant serum in the morning to help defend against UV-triggered free radicals.
