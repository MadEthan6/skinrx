It is the question we hear every spring: "I know I should have started sooner, but is it too late to start laser hair removal before summer?" The honest answer is: it depends on your expectations, and starting sooner still pays off.

How Laser Hair Removal Actually Works

Laser hair removal targets the pigment (melanin) inside the hair follicle with concentrated light energy, disabling the follicle at the root. Because hair grows in cycles and the laser works best during the active growth phase, most patients need a series of 6 to 8 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart for optimal reduction.

That means a complete series spans roughly 6 to 9 months from start to finish. If you begin in late spring, you will not finish all 6 to 8 sessions before July, yet starting still gives you real advantages right away.

What You Can Achieve This Summer

Even after your first 2 to 3 sessions, most patients notice a clear reduction in hair density, slower regrowth, fewer ingrown hairs, and far less time spent shaving. You will feel smoother than before, even while continuing the rest of your series.

What You Need to Know About Sun and Laser Hair Removal

The key summer rule is managing UV exposure around appointments: avoid tanning or direct sun on the treatment area for at least 2 weeks before and after each session. Tanned skin holds extra surface pigment, which makes treatment less comfortable and increases pigment-change risk.

If you have a lake trip or beach vacation planned, simply let our team schedule your sessions cleanly around those dates.

The Best Reason to Start Now

Every session you complete this summer is one fewer session waiting for you next spring. Patients who begin in June or July often wrap up their full series well ahead of the following summer season.
