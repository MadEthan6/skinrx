What "No Downtime" Actually Means

When we say no downtime, we mean treatments where you can return to normal activities like work, errands, and social plans immediately or within 24 hours. No hiding indoors, no heavy peeling, and no waiting a week before anyone sees you.

Here are the best SkinRx treatments to schedule all summer long.

EmFace: Your Summer Facelift Alternative

EmFace is one of the most popular no-downtime treatments we offer. It combines synchronized radiofrequency and HIFES muscle stimulation to lift and tone the face without needles or recovery. Each session takes about 20 minutes, and you can head straight back to lunch, work, or the ballfield afterward.

Most patients complete a series of 4 sessions, fitting easily into a busy summer calendar.

PRX: The No-Peel Peel

Want the smoothing and tightening benefits of a chemical peel without visible flaking in public? PRX is a needle-free biorevitalizing treatment that stimulates collagen and tightens skin with no needles and no visible peeling. You can return to your normal routine and even wear makeup the same day.

A series of 4 sessions ($1,200) spaced 7 days apart delivers optimal remodeling, or you can book a single session ($350) anytime you want an immediate refresh.

Dysport, Xeomin & Letybo: Quick, No Recovery

Neurotoxin treatments have essentially zero downtime. You come in for forehead lines, frown lines, or crow's feet, and leave looking like yourself while smoothing unfolds gradually over 2 to 7 days. Most patients experience no noticeable swelling or interruption to weekend plans.

We carry Dysport ($5/unit), Xeomin ($12/unit), and Letybo ($12/unit), so your provider can match the exact diffusion and onset profile to your goals.

Dermaplaning: Instant Glow, Zero Recovery

For immediate smoothness with zero recovery time, dermaplaning gently removes dead surface buildup and peach fuzz with a sterile blade in about 30 minutes. Skincare absorbs better, makeup sits like silk, and no, peach fuzz does not grow back thicker.

Dermaplaning is available as a $50 add-on to any chemical peel.

Lip Filler: Weekend-Ready

Lip filler involves minimal downtime for most patients: usually just mild swelling for 24 to 48 hours. Many patients schedule on Thursday or Friday and feel comfortable and settled by the weekend. Using Revanesse Versa or Restylane Kysse, our providers create soft, balanced results starting at $700.
