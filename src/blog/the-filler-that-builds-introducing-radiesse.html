What Makes Radiesse Different?

Most fillers on the market are hyaluronic acid-based, adding volume by drawing water into tissue. Radiesse works differently.

Radiesse is composed of calcium hydroxylapatite (CaHA) microspheres suspended in a smooth gel carrier. This dual-action formula delivers immediate structural correction on day one while stimulating your body's natural collagen and elastin production over the weeks and months that follow.

Think of it as a two-phase investment: you leave refreshed the same day, and your body continues building stronger tissue beneath the surface.

What Can Radiesse Treat?

Radiesse is remarkably versatile across multiple facial and off-face areas:

  • Smile Lines & Folds: Effectively softens nasolabial folds, marionette lines, and deeper creases for a naturally refreshed look.
  • Jawline Definition: Creates a cleaner, more sculpted profile and supports early laxity in the lower face.
  • Cheek Volume: Restores youthful mid-face support and structure.
  • Hand Rejuvenation: FDA-approved for the back of the hands to minimize visible veins and tendons for smoother skin.
  • Skin Quality (Hyperdilute Radiesse): When diluted for biostimulation, improves skin thickness, texture, and fine crepiness.
  • Pre-Jowl & Lower Face: Provides structural lift where lower-face tissue has begun to soften without surgery.

Is Radiesse Right for You?

Radiesse is often an excellent fit for patients who:

  • Want longer-lasting results with fewer touch-up appointments
  • Are seeking structural lift and definition in the lower face or jawline
  • Have noticed volume loss on the back of the hands
  • Want to improve overall skin quality, not just add localized fullness
  • Prefer results that evolve naturally over time
  • Have used hyaluronic acid fillers and are ready for biostimulatory support

Note: Radiesse is not recommended for the lips or under-eye areas. A consultation helps confirm the right product for your anatomy and goals.

The Areas You Might Not Know Radiesse Can Treat

Beyond facial contouring, some of the most rewarding applications for Radiesse are in areas that often show aging first:

The Neck: Crepiness, Laxity & "Tech Neck" Lines

Your neck frequently shows aging ahead of your face, and topical creams only go so far. When Radiesse is applied using a hyperdilute biostimulatory technique, it spreads smoothly beneath the skin to stimulate collagen and elastin across the entire neck, thickening crepey tissue and softening horizontal lines over the following months.

The Decollete: Wrinkles, Creasing & Sun Damage

The chest receives heavy cumulative UV exposure and often develops vertical sleep creases and fine lines. Radiesse is FDA-approved for smoothing wrinkles in the decollete, delivering a brighter, firmer, smoother appearance through collagen renewal.

The Power of Pairing: Radiesse + Neuromodulator for the Neck

For patients with both skin laxity and prominent vertical neck bands (platysmal pull), our providers sometimes pair a neuromodulator like Dysport or Xeomin first to relax downward muscular tension, followed two weeks later by hyperdilute Radiesse to rebuild skin firmness. Together, they create a cleaner, smoother neck and jawline profile without surgery.
