Why Fall and Winter Are the Best Seasons for RF Microneedling

It comes down to one major factor: sun exposure. After RF microneedling, your skin is temporarily more sensitive to UV radiation. We recommend avoiding prolonged sun exposure for at least 1 to 2 weeks after each session and wearing broad-spectrum SPF 30+ consistently throughout your series.

In summer, outdoor weekends, lake trips, and prolonged sun can interfere with healing and limit results. In fall and winter, you naturally spend less time in direct UV light, which means smoother recovery, stronger outcomes, and less day-to-day sun management.

There is a practical bonus too: the mild redness you experience for 24 to 48 hours after treatment is much easier to tuck away in sweater weather than at a backyard pool party.

What RF Microneedling Actually Does

RF (radiofrequency) microneedling combines ultra-fine needles that create controlled micro-channels with radiofrequency energy delivered directly into the dermis. This dual approach stimulates collagen at the surface and generates deep tissue tightening from controlled thermal remodeling, producing more dramatic tightening than traditional microneedling alone.

It is one of our strongest treatments for:

  • Skin laxity and sagging, especially along the lower face and jawline
  • Acne scars and textural irregularities
  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Overall skin firmness, pore refinement, and tone
  • Neck laxity (the neck can be added to any session)

How a Fall Series Works

We recommend a series of 3 RF microneedling sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart for optimal results.
