By the time August winds down, most people are looking in the mirror at skin that feels a little dull, a little congested, and a lot less bright than it did in June.

What the Glow Facial Actually Does

At the heart of the Glow Facial is a medical-grade glycolic system from Glytone. Glycolic acid is one of the most studied and trusted exfoliants in skincare, and for good reason: it works by gently dissolving the bonds that hold dead, dull surface cells in place. Clear those away, and the fresh, brighter skin underneath finally gets to show up.

This is not an ordinary spa facial. Because we work with professional-grade glycolic concentrations under medical oversight, the Glow Facial reaches deeper and does more than products you can buy off a shelf. It resurfaces, brightens, and kickstarts your skin's own renewal process, so the glow you walk out with keeps building over the days that follow.

Who It's For

Almost everyone can benefit, and the Glow Facial is especially worth it if you are dealing with any of the post-summer usual suspects:

  • Dullness: that tired, flat look that no amount of highlighter fixes
  • Uneven texture: skin that feels rough or looks bumpy up close
  • Congestion: clogged pores and the occasional breakout from a summer of sweat and sunscreen
  • Early sun damage: the first hints of uneven tone starting to surface

If your skin just looks a little "off" lately, this is very likely the treatment that brings it back.

What to Expect

The Glow Facial is a clinical treatment rather than a passive spa day, though it still feels relaxing. We cleanse, assess your skin, and apply the glycolic system at the strength matched to your skin. You may feel a mild tingle as it works; that is exfoliation doing its job. With little to no downtime, you can return to your normal routine right afterward.

In the days that follow, your skin looks brighter and feels smoother as surface renewal continues. For lasting improvement, the Glow Facial performs especially well as a series, keeping your skin clear and radiant rather than offering only a one-time lift.

Why Do It Under Medical Oversight

Glycolic facials are offered in many places, but not always under the oversight of a licensed medical provider with clinical assessment and professional-grade skincare products backing them up.

That distinction matters. The right glycolic strength depends on your skin type, your history, and your goals. Too gentle and you barely notice a change; too aggressive and you risk irritation. Getting that judgment call right separates a facial that impresses from one that disappoints.

The Perfect Time Is Right Now

The Glow Facial works in any season, and it is especially rewarding right as summer ends and your skin is ready for renewal. It is also a smart first step before planning fall and holiday treatments, much like starting RF microneedling in the fall.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Glow Facial?

A Glow Facial is a professional resurfacing treatment built around a medical-grade glycolic acid system from Glytone. It exfoliates dull surface cells, brightens tone, and stimulates renewal under the oversight of a licensed medical provider.

Is there downtime after a Glow Facial?

Little to none. You may feel a mild tingle during treatment and see slight warmth afterward, and most patients return to their normal day immediately, making it easy before an event or over a lunch break.

How is this different from an at-home glycolic product?

Concentration and clinical oversight. Professional-grade glycolic works at strengths unavailable over the counter, and a provider matches that strength to your skin type and history.

How often should I get a Glow Facial?

It performs best as a series rather than a single visit. Your provider will recommend a cadence based on your skin so your clarity and brightness keep building.

Who is a good candidate?

Most skin types are candidates, and it is particularly effective for dullness, uneven texture, congestion, and early sun damage. We evaluate your skin first to confirm the right level for you.
