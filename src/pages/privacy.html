Privacy, Consultation & Booking Policies

Transparent expectations for aesthetic consultations, scheduling courtesy, Cherry payment plans, and how SkinRx by Burds Family Practice protects your personal information.

Effective Date: September 30, 2024 · Updated for SkinRx by Burds Family Practice, PLLC (Peosta & Dyersville, Iowa)

1. Aesthetic Consultation & Cancellation Policy

SkinRx by Burds Family Practice charges a $75 skin consultation fee for dedicated one-on-one aesthetic assessments. When you move forward with a treatment or medical-grade skincare purchase, that consultation fee applies toward your treatment or product total per clinic practice.

We kindly request at least 24 hours notice if you need to reschedule or cancel an appointment so we can offer that provider time to another patient. Appointments may be managed through your Mangomint confirmation link or by calling (563) 588-6659.

2. Information We Collect

When you book a visit, request a consultation callback, or become an aesthetic patient at our Peosta or Dyersville clinic, we may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, mailing address, email address, phone number, date of birth, and appointment preferences.
  • Clinical & Health Information: Medical history, medication lists, allergy disclosures, treatment plans, consent forms, and clinical before-and-after photography when authorized.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through our payment partners, including Mangomint or Cherry when selected.
  • Technical Information: Standard server or browser request details (such as browser type and device size) needed to deliver this website cleanly.

3. How We Use & Safeguard Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal or health information to third parties. SkinRx uses your information strictly to evaluate candidacy for medical aesthetic services, communicate regarding appointments and follow-up care, process authorized payments, and meet applicable Iowa and federal healthcare privacy obligations.

As the medical aesthetics arm of Burds Family Practice, PLLC, clinical records and treatment charts are handled with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards consistent with medical practice standards and HIPAA privacy expectations.

4. Third-Party Services & Cherry Financing Disclosure

Online scheduling and digital gift cards are hosted by Mangomint, and physician-dispensed SkinBetter Science online orders are fulfilled through our authorized SkinBetter Science partner storefront. Flexible monthly payment plans are offered through Cherry (pay.withcherry.com/burdsfamilypractice). Any financing pre-qualification, credit assessment, APR terms, or loan servicing is managed directly between you and Cherry; SkinRx does not view or store your credit report or banking credentials.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Age Policy

You may request access to, correction of, or updates to your personal contact records, and you may opt out of promotional emails or clinic announcements at any time. SkinRx cosmetic aesthetic services and website forms are intended for adults age 18 and older; we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors online.

6. Medical Disclaimer

All educational articles, treatment comparisons, price ranges, and before-and-after photos on burdsskinrx.com are provided for general informational purposes only. Nothing on this website constitutes individual medical advice, establishes a provider-patient relationship online, or substitutes for an in-person clinical evaluation with a licensed medical provider. Individual results vary based on anatomy, skin type, and adherence to post-care instructions.
