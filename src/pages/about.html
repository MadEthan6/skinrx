Medical Oversight First

Clinical care behind every treatment

Jenny Burds, DNP, ARNP founded Burds Family Practice in Peosta after 25+ years in healthcare and more than a decade as a Family Nurse Practitioner. As the family clinic grew, patients asked for aesthetic and skin treatments guided by the same medical standards they already trusted for primary care.

Unlike standalone day spas or non-medical studios, SkinRx operates under direct medical-director oversight. Our injectors and laser team train alongside leading injectors in New York City, Chicago, Dallas, and Miami, then bring those techniques home to eastern Iowa using vetted platforms like EmFace, Exion, Nirvana laser hair removal, and physician-dispensed SkinBetter Science and Glytone skincare.

Clinical Philosophy

How we plan every visit

Good aesthetic medicine never rushes a decision or leaves you looking overdone. Every appointment follows three straightforward commitments.

Real anatomy first

Every face, jawline, and skin barrier behaves differently. We assess muscle movement, facial volume, skin thickness, and your medical history before recommending a product, device, or dose.
