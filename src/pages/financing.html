Financing Questions

Common questions about Cherry & pricing

Does checking my Cherry eligibility affect my credit score?

No. Cherry uses a soft credit inquiry when you check eligibility and preview payment options, so checking your rate does not impact your credit score.

What can I finance with Cherry at SkinRx?

You can apply Cherry payment plans toward injectables, EmFace, Exion Face, Exion RF microneedling, laser hair removal packages, DE|RIVE hair restoration, signature bundles, and qualifying treatment plans up to $50,000.

Are 0% APR options available?

Yes. 0% APR promotional options are available for qualifying applicants, and standard APR options start at 5.99%. Exact terms depend on your Cherry approval.

Do I have to join a monthly membership at SkinRx?

No. SkinRx does not require recurring spa memberships. You pay per treatment, choose a multi-session series or signature package, or use Cherry when you prefer monthly payments.

Can I apply my $75 consultation fee toward a package or skincare?

Yes. Your $75 skin consultation fee applies toward your chosen treatment plan or medical-grade skincare purchase.
