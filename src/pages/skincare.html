Barrier & Retinoid Basics

A practical AM / PM framework

Consistent morning protection and evening repair prepare your skin for RF microneedling, PRX, and laser visits while protecting long-term results.

Morning: Protect & Defend

Cleanse gently, apply an antioxidant serum to help buffer daily UV and environmental stress, and finish every morning with broad-spectrum mineral SPF such as sunbetter TONE SMART SPF 75.

Evening: Renew & Support

Remove sunscreen and makeup thoroughly, then use a gentle retinoid or glycolic formula such as AlphaRet or Mystro alongside barrier-supporting hydration as directed by your provider.

Pre- & Post-Procedure Care
