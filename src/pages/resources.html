Patient FAQ · 7 Categories

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about injectables, non-surgical lifting devices, peels, weight management, and scheduling at SkinRx in Peosta and Dyersville.

1. Injectables & Tox

What neurotoxin products does SkinRx offer?

We carry three FDA-approved neurotoxins: Dysport ($5/unit), Xeomin ($12/unit), and Letybo ($12/unit). SkinRx intentionally does not carry Botox or Daxxify because these three options let our injectors tailor onset speed, diffusion, and formulation purity (Xeomin contains no accessory proteins) to each patient's anatomy.

What do neurotoxins treat, and how long do results last?

Neurotoxins relax dynamic muscles that cause forehead lines, frown lines ("11s"), and crow's feet, and can also be placed for a subtle Lip Flip ($100). Dysport and Letybo typically begin taking effect in 2 to 3 days and Xeomin in 3 to 5 days. Most patients enjoy smooth, natural expression for 3 to 4 months (and up to 3 to 6 months with Xeomin).

Will I look frozen or overdone after injectables?

No. Our medical providers take a conservative, anatomy-first approach so you look rested and refreshed while keeping your natural facial expressions. We would always rather start thoughtfully and refine at follow-up than over-treat.

2. Fillers & Biostimulators

What hyaluronic acid fillers do you use for lips, cheeks, and contouring?

SkinRx uses trusted hyaluronic acid fillers including Revanesse Versa and Restylane Kysse for natural lip volume and hydration ($700), as well as cheek and facial balancing fillers (typically $850 or priced by personalized plan during consultation). Hyaluronidase filler dissolving is also available by consultation if old filler from elsewhere needs correction.

How are Sculptra and Radiesse different from standard dermal fillers?

Standard hyaluronic acid fillers restore immediate volume for 6 to 18 months. Sculptra stimulates your body's own collagen gradually over several months for subtle fullness lasting up to 2+ years. Radiesse (calcium hydroxylapatite) delivers immediate structural lift in areas like smile lines, jawline, pre-jowl, hands, and neck while also building collagen over 12 to 18+ months.

What is EZ Gel and why is it popular for under-eyes?

EZ Gel is a 100% natural, autologous plasma biostimulating filler created from a small sample of your own blood. Because it contains no synthetic gel, there is no allergy risk, making it an excellent option for delicate under-eye hollows where traditional fillers can sometimes look heavy.

3. Devices: EmFace & Exion

What is EmFace and how does it lift without needles?

EmFace combines synchronized radiofrequency (RF) skin heating with HIFES (High-Intensity Facial Electromagnetic Stimulation) to tone facial elevator muscles and remodel collagen at the same time. A typical series is 4 comfortable, 20-minute sessions with zero needles and zero downtime (2 areas: $2,000 series / $600 single; 3 areas: $3,000 series / $850 single).

How does Exion Face tighten skin differently from older RF devices?

BTL Exion Face pairs monopolar radiofrequency with targeted ultrasound to reach deeper tissue layers and naturally stimulate hyaluronic acid, collagen, and elastin production without needles or downtime. Patients notice firmer skin texture and improved hydration on the face, neck, and body.

4. Microneedling & Laser Hair

What is the difference between traditional microneedling and Exion RF microneedling?

Traditional microneedling ($350 single / $900 series of 3) creates micro-channels in the upper skin to soften texture, pores, and mild acne scars. BTL Exion RF microneedling ($600 single / $1,650 series of 3; neck add-on $50 single / $150 series of 3) adds AI-guided radiofrequency heat to reach deeper collagen layers without harsh pass counts, making it stronger for laxity, deeper lines, and scarring with 24 to 48 hours of mild redness.

How many Nirvana laser hair removal sessions will I need?

Using the Nirvana laser system by Reveal Lasers, most patients complete a series of 6 to 8 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart to catch follicles in their active growth phase. Most patients achieve 80% to 90% permanent hair reduction across face and body areas safely for a wide range of skin types.

6. Weight Loss & Hair Restoration

Does SkinRx offer medically supervised weight loss?

Yes. Because SkinRx operates under Burds Family Practice, qualified patients have access to medically supervised GLP-1 weight management with real nurse practitioner oversight, lab review, and skin-elasticity support rather than unmonitored online clinics.

Who is a candidate for DE|RIVE hair restoration?
