Injectables

We focus on Dysport, Xeomin, and Letybo rather than Botox or Daxxify, alongside hyaluronic acid fillers and collagen-building biostimulators for subtle, natural balance.

Neuromodulators (Wrinkle Relaxers)

  • Dysport $5 / unit

    Fast 2 to 3 day onset with smooth diffusion, well suited for larger areas like the forehead. Lasts 3 to 4 months.

  • Xeomin $12 / unit

    Purified neurotoxin formulated without accessory proteins. 3 to 5 day onset; typically lasts 3 to 6 months.

  • Letybo $12 / unit

    Korea's #1 neuromodulator with fast 2 to 3 day onset and crisp, localized placement.

  • Lip Flip $100

    4 to 6 units of Dysport, Xeomin, or Letybo to gently relax the upper lip border in ~10 minutes. Lasts 6 to 8 weeks.
