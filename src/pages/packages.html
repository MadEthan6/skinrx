Bridal & Milestone Prep

When to start before your wedding or event

Great skin photographs best when collagen and barrier care start ahead of schedule rather than the week before.

  1. 4 to 6 Months Out

    Begin with a $75 consultation, establish a SkinBetter Science routine, and start your 3-session Exion RF Microneedling or EmFace series so collagen rebuilds steadily.

  2. 8 to 12 Weeks Out

    Complete facial balancing fillers or Exion Face tightening and move into your customized chemical peel or PRX series to even tone and refine pores.

  3. 2 to 4 Weeks Out

    Schedule Dysport, Xeomin, or Letybo 2 to 4 weeks ahead for settled expression lines, followed by Dermaplaning, a Mandelic Red Carpet Peel, and Beaming White ($150) within 10 days of photos.
