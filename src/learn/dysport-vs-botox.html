Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Dysport and Botox?

Dysport and Botox both use botulinum toxin type A to temporarily relax facial muscles, but they differ in formulation and behavior. Dysport has a faster onset (often visible in 2 to 3 days) and diffuses more broadly, making it well-suited for larger treatment areas like the forehead. Botox has a slower onset of 3 to 5 days and stays more localized. SkinRx carries Dysport rather than Botox because of its clinical performance and patient value.

Why doesn't SkinRx carry Botox?

SkinRx does not carry Botox primarily because Botox commands a significant price premium that ultimately gets passed on to patients. After carefully evaluating available neurotoxins, our team determined that Dysport, Xeomin, and Letybo deliver excellent clinical results and stronger patient value without sacrificing quality or safety.

What is Xeomin and how is it different from Botox or Dysport?

Xeomin is often called the "naked toxin" because it contains only the pure botulinum toxin molecule with no accessory proteins. The body is less likely to develop antibodies over time, which helps maintain effectiveness across repeated treatments. Xeomin is a great choice for patients with sensitive systems, those who have noticed fading response to other toxins, or anyone who prefers a pure, localized formulation.

What is Letybo and why does SkinRx carry it?

Letybo is the newest FDA-approved neurotoxin in the U.S. after years as the top-selling toxin in South Korea. It features a low protein load, a precise diffusion pattern that does not spread like Dysport, and a fast onset of 2 to 3 days. SkinRx carries Letybo for targeted treatment areas requiring controlled placement.

Why doesn't SkinRx carry Daxxify?

Daxxify was marketed for extended duration up to 6 months, but in clinical practice it has not consistently delivered on that promise, with market adoption remaining below 5%. SkinRx determined it did not meet our standard for patient value and predictable outcomes.

Which neurotoxin is right for me: Dysport, Xeomin, or Letybo?

The right choice depends on your anatomy, treatment area, and goals. Dysport is often preferred for larger areas like the forehead where broader diffusion helps create a smooth finish. Xeomin suits patients seeking a pure formulation with minimal protein load and precise results. Letybo is ideal for targeted areas where controlled, non-spreading placement matters most. Your SkinRx provider will recommend the best fit during your consultation.
