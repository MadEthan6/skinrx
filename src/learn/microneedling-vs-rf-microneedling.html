The main difference between traditional microneedling and RF microneedling is depth and heat. Traditional microneedling creates controlled micro-injuries at the skin's surface to trigger your natural collagen response. RF microneedling does the same, then delivers radiofrequency energy through the needles into the deeper dermis, layering heat-driven collagen stimulation onto the mechanical response. The result is deeper remodeling, stronger tightening, and faster visible improvement at a higher price point. SkinRx offers both, because not every patient needs the most intensive option to achieve their goals.

How Each Treatment Works

Both treatments start with the same foundation. The difference is how deep they reach and what happens below the surface.

Traditional · Collagen Induction Therapy

Traditional Microneedling

A precision pen creates thousands of microscopic channels across the skin surface, activating your body's natural wound-healing response to build new collagen and elastin.

  1. Fine needles create controlled micro-channels at the surface.
  2. Your body detects the micro-injuries and initiates healing.
  3. Collagen and elastin production increases naturally.
  4. Micro-channels enhance absorption of topical serums applied during treatment.
  5. Skin gradually refines in texture and tone over 4 to 8 weeks.
BTL Exion · AI-Driven RF Microneedling

RF Microneedling

Combines traditional micro-channels with radiofrequency heat delivered directly into the deep dermis, triggering immediate collagen contraction and deeper remodeling.

  1. Fine needles create micro-channels, just like traditional microneedling.
  2. Radiofrequency energy is delivered through the needles into the deep dermis.
  3. Thermal energy produces immediate collagen contraction and early tightening.
  4. Deeper thermal remodeling drives longer-lasting structural firmness.
  5. BTL Exion AI monitors skin temperature in real time to optimize safety and comfort.

Choosing Between the Two

The right answer depends on what you want to improve, how quickly you want results, and your budget.

Traditional Microneedling

A great fit if you are addressing

  • Uneven skin texture or tone
  • Mild acne scarring
  • Fine lines and early wrinkles
  • Enlarged pores or dull complexion
  • Maintenance between deeper RF treatments
  • A lower investment per session or first-time collagen treatment
RF Microneedling (BTL Exion)

The stronger choice if you are addressing

  • Skin laxity or early sagging along the lower face and jawline
  • Deeper wrinkles and folds
  • Significant or pitted acne scarring
  • Crepey skin on the neck or body
  • Fewer total sessions for deeper structural tightening

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between microneedling and RF microneedling?

Traditional microneedling uses fine needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin, triggering natural collagen production. RF microneedling does the same thing while also delivering radiofrequency energy through the needles into the deeper dermis, adding heat-driven collagen stimulation for deeper remodeling and improved skin tightening.

Which is better: microneedling or RF microneedling?

Neither is universally better because they address different depths and budgets. Traditional microneedling is ideal for texture, tone, mild scarring, and fine lines with lower cost per session. RF microneedling is stronger for skin laxity, deeper wrinkles, and more significant scarring in fewer sessions.

How many sessions of microneedling do I need?

Most patients see optimal results from traditional microneedling after 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4 weeks apart. RF microneedling typically requires 3 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart for similar or greater improvement.

Does microneedling hurt?

Traditional microneedling is generally well-tolerated with topical numbing cream applied beforehand. RF microneedling adds gentle heat from radiofrequency energy, though BTL Exion uses AI-driven energy delivery to monitor skin response in real time and keep treatment far more comfortable than older RF platforms.

Can microneedling be used on all skin types?

Yes. Both traditional microneedling and RF microneedling are generally safe across all skin types and tones because neither relies on light energy that targets melanin.
