Frequently Asked Questions

Can an esthetician in Iowa do Botox or fillers?

No. In Iowa, injectables such as Botox, Dysport, and dermal fillers must be administered by or under the direct supervision of a licensed medical professional, typically a physician, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant. Estheticians are not licensed to perform injections.

Does it matter who trained my injector?

Yes, significantly. Injectable aesthetics is technique-driven. The same product can produce very different results depending on placement, dosing, and anatomical judgment. Hands-on training in high-volume aesthetic centers directly shapes natural-looking outcomes.
